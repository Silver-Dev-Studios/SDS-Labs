Standing orders — Lab protocols

Three rules. Sort of.

P-01

Why does it exist?

Anything in SDS Labs may not have a reason why it was made. That is acceptable. Projects may be deleted, or they may simply never change again.

P-02

Why is nothing updated?

Lab projects are side projects. They are not treated as big projects by SDS and are made for no particular reason — so they update when someone feels like it, not before.

P-03

What if it gets big?

If a project is actively used and the community wants more of it, it gets promoted: separated from SDS Labs and graduated into a real Silver Dev Studios product.

Specimen progression — Lab lifecycle

From lab bench to real product

  1. A

    Curiosity

    Someone in the studio has an idea with no business case. It lands in a lab vault.

  2. B

    Ignored / loved

    The idea either collects dust or collects users. Both outcomes are valid.

  3. C

    Promotion

    If the community actively uses it, it graduates into a standalone SDS product and leaves the lab forever.

  4. D

    Retirement

    If it stays ignored, it is archived or quietly deleted. Missing specimens are not investigated.
