## 🧫 Why?

Anything in SDS Labs may not have a reason why it was made. That is acceptable. Projects may be **deleted**, or they may simply **never change again**.

## 🔧 Why aren't projects being updated?

Lab projects are side projects. They are not treated as big projects by SDS and are made for no particular reason — so they update when someone feels like it, not before.

## 🚀 What if a project starts becoming big?

If a project is actively being used and the community wants more of it, it gets promoted: separated from SDS Labs and graduated into a real Silver Dev Studios product.

---

## 🗺️ Lab Lifecycle

```
Curiosity ──► Ignored / loved ──► Promotion  (graduated into an SDS product)
                    │
                    └────────────► Retirement (archived or quietly deleted)
```

1. **Curiosity** — someone in the studio has an idea with no business case. It lands in a lab vault.
2. **Ignored / loved** — the idea either collects dust or collects users. Both outcomes are valid.
3. **Promotion** — if the community actively uses it, it becomes a standalone SDS product and leaves the lab forever.
4. **Retirement** — if it stays ignored, it is archived or deleted. Missing specimens are not investigated.

## ⚠️ Standing Orders

- Anything in this lab may not have a reason for existing.
- Projects may be deleted without notice — or never updated. Forever.
- Everything here is made for fun, not as a maintained product.
- Liability is limited to the lab floor. Enter at your own curiosity.
