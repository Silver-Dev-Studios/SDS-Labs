# SDS-Labs
Random projects with no business purpose made by Silver Dev Studios


# Why?
Anything in SDS Labs may not have a reason why it was made. Projects may be deleted or not changed.

# Why aren't projects being updated?
Projects made for SDS Labs are side projects and aren't noticed as a big project by SDS and made for no particular reason.

# What if a project starts becoming big?
If a project is being actively being used and the community wants more of that project, the project will be come separate from SDS Labs and will become an actual product of SDS.
